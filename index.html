better-flow: an idea to distribute water better

The idea: help Tunisian farmers use less water and spend less money with a better layout of irrigation networks.

31.25%lower costs for the farmer, according to the project’s estimate

The problem

Water is poorly distributed across plots. The result: waste, leaks and higher bills for the farmer.

The solution

A SaaS tool that computes the shortest, most efficient pipe layout, inspired by nature through the BLOB algorithm.

The market

Farms over 50 hectares (B2B), the Ministry of Agriculture, SONEDE and OTD (B2G). Competitors studied: IRRICAD and WCADI.
